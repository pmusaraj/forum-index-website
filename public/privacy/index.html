Privacy policy

Updated October 6, 2026

Information we collect

When you enable contributions, we store an installation identifier, display and device names, verification status, and submitted reads, stars, votes, and reports. Feedback includes your message, installation association, and app/device software versions.

We also index public forum titles, author names, excerpts, links, and engagement counts.

How we use it

Reads and stars personalize your For You feed. Contributions improve topic ranking and review, verification helps prevent abuse, and feedback helps improve the app. Installation identifiers are pseudonymous, not anonymous.

Retention

Daily cleanup deletes topics older than 60 days from publication, including their linked reading and contribution history. Feedback is deleted after 180 days through daily cleanup. Installation records remain until erased. Operational records and backups follow operator-managed retention schedules.

Service providers

Apple DeviceCheck receives a device verification token, which we do not retain. When AI classification is enabled, our configured AI provider receives public topic excerpts and forum metadata—not your reading history or app feedback. Hosting and other providers may process data to operate the service.

Your choices

Stopping contributions disables your installation credential but retains existing data.

Deleting your installation data removes its names, credential, reading and contribution history, linked feedback, and invitation from the live application database. It also removes its identifier from forum submission notes. Other installations and public forum content remain unaffected. You can enroll again as a new installation.

Deletion limits

Deletion does not remove copies in backups, operational logs, your device, source forums, or service-provider systems, or reset Apple’s DeviceCheck state. Apps should clear local data after deletion.
